## Consensus Top 5 (Cross-Expert Average)

### 1. v14-conversion-optimized — AVG 8.1
The most complete page from top to bottom. Outcome-oriented hero ("Find Your Next Signing in Seconds, Not Days"), inline email capture with "no credit card" microcopy, 3-phase pipeline visualization, FAQ objection handling with 30-day money-back guarantee, and competitive comparison table. Best CTA strategy and friction reduction.

### 2. v13-minimal-variant3 — AVG 7.9
Best balance across all dimensions. Highest information density with lowest cognitive load. Hero packs headline + testimonial + SOC2 badge + trust logos in one viewport. Strongest security/compliance section (audit logs, SSO, GDPR). The variation most likely to survive a board-level procurement review.

### 3. v08-minimal-variant2 — AVG 7.7
Best scannability. "Close the Gap Between Data and Decisions" is the most strategically sharp headline. "Find the needle. Skip the haystack." is the most memorable copy. Competitive comparison table makes the value undeniable at a glance. League logo strip communicates scope instantly.

### 4. v04-minimal-variant — AVG 7.6
Best for technical buyers. Hero leads with hard metrics (<0.8s latency, 99.2% accuracy). "Operational Control Center" framing is infrastructure-grade. Most detailed and procurement-friendly pricing table. Strongest brand consistency with the "Analytical Architect" personality.

### 5. v03-trust-first-v2 — AVG 7.6
Best trust architecture. BYOK encryption flow diagram is the single best security element across all variations — something a sporting director can screenshot for IT/board approval. Three named testimonials, visible pricing, integration partner logos.
