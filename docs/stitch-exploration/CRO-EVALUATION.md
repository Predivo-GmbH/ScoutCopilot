# ScoutCopilot Landing Page CRO Evaluation

**Evaluator:** Senior CRO Specialist
**Date:** 2026-03-23
**Scope:** 14 landing page variations scored on conversion optimization fundamentals

---

## Scoring Criteria (1-10 each)

| Code | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **TS** | Trust Signals — logos, badges, compliance, security language |
| **SP** | Social Proof — testimonials, case studies, named clients, stats |
| **PP** | Pricing Presentation — clarity, anchoring, tier differentiation |
| **CTA** | CTA Strategy — placement, copy, urgency, friction cues |
| **FR** | Friction Reduction — objection handling, FAQ, risk reversal, simplicity |

## TOP 3 Picks and Rationale

### #1: v15-trust-first-v3 (7.4)

**Why this wins:** This is the only variation that puts a real testimonial *inside the hero section*. For a B2B buyer spending $149-$599/mo, the first 5 seconds determine whether they scroll or bounce. Seeing "Sevilla FC -- Verified Recruitment Partner" with a quote next to the headline immediately answers the two critical questions: "Is this real?" and "Do people like me use this?" The white/light color scheme is a strategic advantage -- it reads as institutional and enterprise-grade rather than startup-y. The named club logos (Sevilla, RB Leipzig, Benfica, Ajax, Inter Miami) are the strongest logo bar across all 14 variations. The dual CTA at the bottom ("Start Free Trial" + "Talk to Sales") correctly segments the self-serve Scout buyer from the enterprise Club buyer.

### #2: v03-trust-first-v2 (7.4)

**Why this is essential:** Has the single best security section of any variation -- the BYOK encryption flow diagram visually demonstrates data architecture in a way that a sporting director can screenshot and show to their IT department or board. Three named testimonials with photos and titles provide diverse social proof. Pricing is well-structured with integration partner logos. This variation does the best job of addressing the #1 B2B SaaS objection (data security) while maintaining strong conversion elements.

### #3: v14-conversion-optimized (6.8)

**Why this matters:** Has the lowest-friction conversion path of any variation -- email capture directly in the hero with clear "no credit card, 14-day trial, cancel anytime" microcopy. The FAQ section with a 30-day money-back guarantee is the only variation to offer explicit risk reversal. The competitor comparison table is the best "why us" argument across all variations. This variation would likely have the highest trial-start rate, even if it needs more trust signals to close enterprise deals.

## Recommended Final Design Architecture

Based on this analysis, the highest-converting landing page would combine:

1. **Hero:** v15's testimonial-in-hero pattern + v14's inline email capture + v08's "no credit card" microcopy
2. **Problem section:** v05's "Your Wyscout Data Is Sitting There. Unused." agitation
3. **Live demo:** v06's interactive query demonstration
4. **Trust/Security:** v03's BYOK encryption flow diagram + v13's compliance feature list
5. **Social proof:** v12's Sevilla FC "70% to 100%" testimonial + v02's FC Utrecht case study
6. **Pricing:** v07's monthly/yearly toggle + v04's feature comparison table
7. **Objection handling:** v14's FAQ with 30-day money-back guarantee + v08's comparison table
8. **Final CTA:** v15's dual "Start Free Trial" + "Talk to Sales" segmentation + v1's FOMO closer
