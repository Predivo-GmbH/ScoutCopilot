## TOP 3 Picks

### 1st Place: v13-minimal-variant3 (AVG 8.6)

**Why:** This variant achieves the highest information density per pixel with the lowest cognitive load -- the holy grail of B2B landing page design. The white background with blue accents creates the best contrast ratios for accessibility (WCAG compliance would be easiest here). The hero is doing triple duty: headline + testimonial + trust logos in a single scroll. The compliance section (SOC2, SSO, audit logs) directly addresses the #1 objection of enterprise buyers. The user flow from trust > problem > features > compliance > capabilities > pricing is a textbook enterprise SaaS funnel. The clean, light design also communicates "institutional" rather than "startup" -- exactly right for scouting departments at professional clubs.

### 2nd Place: v04-minimal-variant (AVG 8.4)

**Why:** For the technically-minded buyer (Head of Analytics, Technical Director), this variant speaks their language immediately. Leading with hard metrics (<0.8s latency, 99.2% accuracy, 420+ leagues) in the hero skips the fluff and goes straight to credibility. The "Operational Control Center" framing with detailed capability cards positions ScoutCopilot as infrastructure rather than a toy. The feature-comparison pricing table is the most detailed and procurement-friendly of all variants. This is the variant most likely to survive a technical evaluation committee.

### 3rd Place: v08-minimal-variant2 (AVG 8.2) / v12-bold-editorial-v2 (AVG 8.2) -- tie

**v08 wins on:** Scannability and cognitive load. The headline, league logos, and comparison table create a 3-second understanding of the product. The "Find the needle. Skip the haystack." copy is the most memorable line across all 14 variants. Best for a time-poor Head of Recruitment who needs to grok the value instantly.

**v12 wins on:** Emotional resonance and storytelling. The editorial typography, player heatmaps, and real testimonial from a named LaLiga technical director create the most compelling narrative. The numbered feature walkthrough is the most elegant. Best for buyers who need to be convinced, not just informed.

## Recommended Final Design Blueprint

Combine the best elements into a single page structure:

1. **Hero:** v13's layout (headline + testimonial + trust logos) with v14's inline email capture and v06's NL query demo
2. **Problem section:** v08's "Find the needle" copywriting with v05's pain quantification
3. **Features:** v09's 4-card capability suite with v12's numbered walkthrough style
4. **Trust/Security:** v03's BYOK encryption flow + v13's compliance badges (SOC2, SSO)
5. **Social proof:** v12's named testimonial + v04's performance metrics strip
6. **Pricing:** v04's feature-comparison table format with v08's clean card layout
7. **Final CTA:** v15's dual-CTA (self-serve + sales) with v01's urgency framing
8. **Footer:** v13's clean 4-column layout
