## 1. Pricing Tiers

### Tier 1 — Scout (Entry)

| | |
|---|---|
| **Price** | $149/mo (billed monthly) or $129/mo (billed annually = $1,548/yr) |
| **Who it's for** | Small agencies, individual senior scouts at Tier 3-4 clubs, clubs evaluating whether AI scouting adds value |

**Features included:**
- Connect 1 data source (Wyscout OR StatsBomb API credentials)
- Natural language player search (unlimited queries)
- AI-generated player shortlists (up to 25 shortlists/mo)
- Basic player comparison (side-by-side, up to 3 players)
- AI scouting reports — 10 per month
- Single-league focus (1 league active at a time)
- 1 user seat

**What's missing (pushing toward Pro):**
- No multi-league search
- No tactical fit analysis
- No export/PDF reports
- Limited shortlists and scouting reports
- Single user only

### Tier 2 — Pro (Main Tier)

| | |
|---|---|
| **Price** | $299/mo (billed monthly) or $249/mo (billed annually = $2,988/yr) |
| **Who it's for** | Professional clubs (Tier 2-4), established player agencies — the core target customer |

**Features included:**
- Connect both data sources (Wyscout AND StatsBomb simultaneously)
- Natural language player search (unlimited queries)
- AI-generated player shortlists (unlimited)
- Advanced player comparison (up to 10 players, radar charts, percentile rankings)
- AI scouting reports — unlimited
- Tactical system fit analysis ("How does this player fit our 4-3-3 press?")
- Multi-league search (search across ALL leagues in your API subscription)
- Development trajectory prediction ("Where will this player be in 2 years?")
- PDF export of all reports and shortlists (club-branded)
- Saved search templates and watchlists
- 3 user seats (e.g., Head of Recruitment + 2 scouts)

**Why this is the obvious choice:**
- The Scout tier is deliberately limited to feel constraining — 10 reports/month and 1 league is not enough for any serious scouting operation
- At $299/mo, this tier costs less than 1% of a junior analyst's salary ($40K-60K/yr = $3,333-5,000/mo) while doing 80% of the same work
- Tactical fit analysis and development trajectory — the two strongest differentiators — are only available here
- Unlimited reports and shortlists remove all friction from daily scouting workflow
- 3 seats let the entire scouting department use it, not just one person

## 2. The Irresistible Offer Hook

### Primary hook:

**"Stop drowning in spreadsheets. Ask your Wyscout data a question in plain English and get an AI-ranked shortlist in 30 seconds — not 3 hours."**

### Alternative hooks (test in marketing):

- "Your club pays thousands for Wyscout data. Our AI turns it from a filing cabinet into a scouting department."
- "A junior analyst costs EUR 40K/year and takes 3 hours per shortlist. Scout Copilot costs EUR 249/month and takes 30 seconds."
- "Same Wyscout data. Same StatsBomb metrics. 100x faster answers. The AI scouting layer Hudl hasn't built yet."

### Rationale:

The Phase 1 research surfaced two dominant pain expressions: (1) the time drain of manual filtering ("hours to sift through reports") and (2) data overload without actionable intelligence ("constantly updating spreadsheets, looking for scattered files"). The primary hook hits both — it names the pain (spreadsheets), names the transformation (plain English question to ranked shortlist), and quantifies the speed difference (30 seconds vs. 3 hours).

## 3. Objection Busters

### Objection 1: "How can an AI tool match the judgment of an experienced scout?"

**Response:** It doesn't replace your scouts — it eliminates the 80% of their work that isn't judgment. Your scouts spend most of their time filtering databases, cross-referencing stats, and building spreadsheets before they even start evaluating players. Scout Copilot handles that entire pre-screening phase in seconds. Your experienced scouts then apply their judgment to a pre-filtered shortlist of 10-15 strong candidates instead of manually grinding through 500. They make better decisions because they spend their time watching video and evaluating fit — not formatting spreadsheets.

**Supporting evidence:** Sevilla FC's head of recruitment confirmed this exact workflow: "Previously it cost scouts hours or longer to sift through reports; now they get a list of players plus a summary in seconds." The scouts still make the final call. The AI just gets them to that point faster.

---

### Objection 2: "We already have Wyscout/StatsBomb — why pay for another tool on top?"

**Response:** You are paying thousands per year for Wyscout/StatsBomb data access. Right now, your team queries it through manual search filters, exports to Excel, and builds comparisons by hand. That is like buying a Ferrari and driving it in first gear. Scout Copilot is the AI layer that turns your existing data investment into an actual competitive advantage.

Consider: your Wyscout subscription gives you the same data that every other club has access to. The clubs winning the transfer market are not the ones with better data — they are the ones who can process it faster and find patterns others miss. Natural language AI queries, tactical fit analysis, and development trajectory predictions are capabilities that no current Wyscout or StatsBomb interface provides. You are not paying for data you already have. You are paying for intelligence you currently cannot extract.

---

### Objection 3: "What if the AI makes mistakes or gives bad recommendations?"

**Response:** Three safeguards. First, Scout Copilot is transparent: every recommendation shows the underlying data, the metrics used, and why each player ranked where they did. There is no black box — your analysts can verify every output against the raw Wyscout/StatsBomb data they already know. Second, the tool is an assistant, not an authority. It generates shortlists and reports; your scouting team makes decisions. It is designed to be challenged and refined — ask follow-up questions, adjust criteria, re-run with different parameters. Third, the AI improves with use. At the Club tier, custom models learn your club's specific playing philosophy and evaluation criteria over time. The more your team uses it, the more aligned it becomes with how your club thinks about recruitment.

Contrast this with the current alternative: a junior analyst manually building comparisons in Excel at 3am before a transfer deadline, possibly missing candidates or making formula errors that no one catches. The AI is not perfect, but it is consistent, tireless, and auditable.

## 4. Launch Pricing Strategy

### 4.1 Annual Pre-Pay Incentive

Annual pre-pay gets an additional month free:

| Tier | Monthly | Annual (pay 11, get 12) | Effective Monthly |
|------|---------|-------------------------|-------------------|
| Scout | $149/mo | $1,639/yr | $136.58/mo |
| Pro | $299/mo | $3,289/yr | $274.08/mo |
| Club | $599/mo | $6,589/yr | $549.08/mo |

### 4.2 Lifetime Deal — NO

Do not offer a lifetime deal. Reasons:
- The product has ongoing per-customer AI API costs ($2-5/mo) that never go away
- Football data is seasonal — customers use this tool continuously, not as a one-time purchase
- LTDs attract bargain hunters, not the professional clubs and agencies who are the real target market
- A $599 LTD would take 2-4 months to break even per customer and then generate losses forever
- The addressable market is only 3,000-5,000 organizations — cannot afford to convert high-value customers to zero-revenue accounts

### 4.3 Transfer Window Alignment

- Launch beta 8-10 weeks before the summer transfer window (launch mid-April for the June-August window)
- Messaging: "Get your scouting AI operational before the summer window opens"
- Football clubs make purchasing decisions in preparation for transfer windows — this is the natural buying cycle

### Key Pricing Decisions and Rationale

1. **$149 entry (not $79):** The Phase 1 report suggested $79/mo. This is too low. Professional clubs and agencies do not trust cheap tools for critical scouting decisions. At $79/mo, the product signals "toy" not "professional tool." Competitors charge $3K-100K/yr. Even at $299/mo ($3,588/yr), Scout Copilot is the cheapest professional-grade option in the market by a factor of 3-10x. Pricing at $149 entry still positions us as affordable while signaling quality.

2. **$299 Pro as the anchor:** This price point sits in a strategic gap — far below competitors (Wyscout $3K-10K/yr for data alone, SciSports $15K-50K/yr) but high enough to signal professional-grade capability. The comparison that matters is not "is $299/mo expensive?" but "is $299/mo cheaper than a junior analyst?" The answer is obviously yes, by 10-15x.

3. **$599 Club as the ceiling (for now):** Enterprise pricing above $599/mo requires enterprise sales motions (demos, procurement, legal review) that a solo founder cannot support at scale. Keep the self-service ceiling at $599 and raise it only when the product and team can support true enterprise sales.

4. **Annual discounts are modest (17%):** The goal is recurring revenue predictability, not discount-driven volume. A 17% annual discount (pay 10 months, get 12) rewards commitment without destroying unit economics.
